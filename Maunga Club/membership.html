To join as a club member, please follow these steps:

  • Proof of address (e.g., copy of power bill)
  • Proof of ID (driver’s licence or passport)
  • Email: maungaclub@maungaclub.maungaclub
  • Your membership will be confirmed by email.
  • Additional information about the Club and accommodation
  • (membership letter) will be sent by regular mail.
