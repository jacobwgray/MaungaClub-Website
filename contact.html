Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join Maunga Club?

To join Maunga Club, please follow these steps:

  1. Email us your full name, physical address, proof of address (e.g., copy of power bill), and proof of ID (driver's license or passport) to maungaclub@maungaclub.maungaclub.
  2. Your membership will be confirmed by email.
  3. Additional information about the club and accommodation (membership letter) will be sent by regular mail.

What are the available chalets?

Maunga Club offers three chalets:

  • Kākāpō Lodge: Accommodates up to 10 adults and 30 children. Features a large kitchen, dining, and living area with great amenities and ski-in and ski-out access.
  • Pūkeko: Accommodates up to 6 adults and 15 children. Ideal for large groups/families looking for exclusive chalet use.
  • Kererū: Accommodates up to 2 adults and 4 children. Perfect for families with 2-4 children, featuring 2 bedrooms and a separate living area.

If you have any other questions, please feel free to email us at maungaclub@maungaclub.maungaclub.
