Kākāpō Lodge

Kākāpō Lodge is the largest of our chalets, offering spacious shared accommodation for Club members. With room for up to 10 adults and 30 children, it's the perfect choice for large groups or events. Some of the key features include a large kitchen, dining, and living area, great amenities, and ski-in and ski-out access. Whether you're planning a family reunion, a team-building retreat, or a gathering of friends, Kākāpō Lodge provides the space and comfort you need for an unforgettable mountain getaway.

Kererū Lodge

Kererū Lodge offers cozy shared accommodation, making it a great choice for families with 2-4 children. With 2 bedrooms and a separate living area, it's the perfect option for a family retreat in the mountains. Whether you're looking to enjoy the serene surroundings, go skiing with your loved ones, or simply relax, Kererū Lodge provides a comfortable and welcoming space for your mountain getaway.

Pūkeko Lodge

Pūkeko Lodge is an excellent choice for large groups or families seeking a chalet to themselves. With room for up to 6 adults and 15 children, it provides comfortable and spacious shared accommodation. The limits on the number of adults and children ensure a pleasant stay for all guests. Whether you're planning a family gathering or a group adventure, Pūkeko Lodge offers the ideal space for your mountain retreat.
