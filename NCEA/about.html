About Maunga Club

Welcome to Maunga Club, where adventure meets comfort in the heart of the mountains. Our exclusive chalets provide the perfect base for your winter getaway. At Maunga Club, we are committed to providing you with the ultimate alpine experience. Whether you're an avid skier or a snowboarding enthusiast, our club is your gateway to endless adventures on the slopes.

Important Information:

  • Only Club members can access our chalets.
  • All chalet bookings must be from Friday to Sunday.
  • Our chalets offer shared accommodation for between 6 and 40 people per chalet.
  • All three chalets are located on the mountain, allowing you to ski to and from the chalets.
  • Our mountain offers 550 hectares of skiing/snowboarding area and the longest ski/snowboard season in New Zealand.

More About Us

At Maunga Club, we have a rich history of providing memorable mountain experiences for our members. Our story began over two decades ago when a group of passionate skiers and snowboarders came together with a shared dream - to create a haven for winter enthusiasts high up in the mountains.

Since then, we've grown into a tight-knit community of adventure-seekers who call Maunga Club their second home. Our chalets have witnessed countless stories of laughter, friendship, and adrenaline-fueled escapades.

Our commitment to preserving the beauty of our natural surroundings is unwavering. We work tirelessly to ensure that our activities have minimal impact on the environment so that future generations can enjoy these pristine slopes.

Join us on this exciting journey, and become a part of our ever-growing Maunga Club family. Experience the thrill of conquering the slopes, the warmth of our mountain hospitality, and the joy of creating memories that last a lifetime.
